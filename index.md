# Privacy Policy for PendulumTimer

**Effective Date:** November 2025
**Developer:** M.H. Studios

## Introduction

PendulumTimer ("the App") is a meditation and focus timer application developed by M.H. Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Session Data (Stored Locally)
The App collects and stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- **Session start time**: When you start a timer session
- **Session end time**: When you complete a timer session
- **Session duration**: How long each session lasted

**Important**: This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties. You can export this data as a CSV file for your personal use.

### 2. In-App Purchase Information
If you purchase the Pro upgrade:
- Purchase transactions are processed by Google Play
- We do not collect or store payment information
- Google Play handles all payment processing securely
- We only receive confirmation that you own the Pro upgrade

### 3. Advertising Data (Free Users Only)
The App displays advertisements using Google AdMob for free users. AdMob may collect:
- Device information (device type, operating system)
- Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)
- Approximate location (based on IP address)
- Advertising ID

**Note**: Pro users do not see ads and this data is not collected for Pro users.

## How We Use Your Information

### Session Data
- Displayed in the Statistics screen to show your focus history
- Used to calculate total time focused and longest session
- Can be exported by you as a CSV file
- Never shared with anyone

### Purchase Data
- Used only to verify your Pro upgrade status
- Managed entirely by Google Play Billing

### Advertising Data
- Used by Google AdMob to display relevant advertisements
- Helps support the free version of the App
- Managed by Google's advertising services

## Data Storage and Security

- **Session data** is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database
- **No cloud storage**: We do not upload your data to any servers
- **No account required**: The App does not require registration or login
- **Data deletion**: Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored data

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

### Google AdMob (Advertising)
- **Purpose**: Display ads to free users
- **Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Data collected**: Device info, ad interactions, approximate location

### Google Play Billing
- **Purpose**: Process in-app purchases
- **Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Data collected**: Purchase transactions (managed by Google)

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is rated for general audiences and does not contain age-restricted content.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access your data**: View your session statistics in the App
- **Export your data**: Use the "Export Statistics" feature to download your data as CSV
- **Delete your data**: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data
- **Opt out of ads**: Upgrade to Pro to remove all advertisements

## Data Retention

- **Session data**: Retained locally on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App
- **Purchase data**: Retained by Google Play as long as you own the Pro upgrade
